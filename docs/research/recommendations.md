# Phase 1 — Recommendations: the guided discovery process

Derived from the [inventory](resource-inventory.md),
[synthesis](synthesis.md) and [gap analysis](gap-analysis.md). This defines
the *product* the site should be; [docs/plan.md](../plan.md) defines how to
build it.

## 1. Positioning

**An honest, deep, self-guided ikigai journey: staged exercises, saved
progress, a synthesis artifact, and a retest loop — grounded in the original
Japanese conception, with the Western Venn offered as an optional, clearly
labelled livelihood lens.**

Design principles, each traceable to research:
1. **Plural, small sources** — never ask for "The One Answer" (Kamiya, Mogi,
   Mitsuhashi).
2. **Feeling-first** — exercises target experiencing (ikigai-kan), not just
   discovering (Kamiya; Mogi pillars 4–5).
3. **Needs are actionable** — Kamiya's 7(+1) needs double as a life-design
   checklist.
4. **Action before insight** — every stage ends in a small real-world action
   (Morita therapy; Mitsuhashi).
5. **Measure honestly** — Ikigai-9 baseline and retest (only validated
   instrument in English).
6. **Correct the record** — teach the Venn's true origin; use it only as an
   optional lens (synthesis §3.1–3.2).
7. **Data stays on-device** — localStorage + export/import; no accounts, no
   analytics (G7).

## 2. Journey structure: 8 stages

Each stage: **teach card(s)** (short, sourced) → **2–5 exercises** →
**stage artifact** (persisted, reused later) → **one small real-world action**
("between sessions"). Estimated total: 6–10 sessions of 20–40 min.

### Stage 0 — Orientation & baseline
- **Purpose:** set expectations; measure the start line.
- **Teach:** what ikigai is/means (etymology); what it is not (not a Venn, not
  Okinawan, not a career quiz); the evidence note (cohort studies,
  association-level honesty); how the journey works and where data lives.
- **Exercises:** expectations check (3 questions); **Ikigai-9 baseline**
  (9 slider items, auto-scored by subscale — emotional, growth, contribution).
- **Outputs:** baseline score card (per-subscale); journey expectations note.
- **Action:** none (this stage *is* the action).

### Stage 1 — Mine the past
- **Purpose:** recover clues; ikigai is often visible early (Mitsuhashi).
- **Teach:** why the past is evidence; memory as data, not nostalgia.
- **Exercises:** timeline of remembered peak moments (5–10 entries: age,
  activity, what it felt like); "as a child I loved…" sentence stems
  (positivepsychology set); lost-loves audit (activities dropped due to
  life circumstances, not disinterest).
- **Outputs:** peak-moments timeline; candidate-themes list (auto-grouped
  keywords the user tags).
- **Action:** ask one person who knew you young: "what was I always doing?"

### Stage 2 — Map the present
- **Purpose:** locate current sources and energy; here-and-now practice.
- **Teach:** Mogi's pillars; flow; joy of little things.
- **Exercises:** 7-day (or today) energy audit — activities vs energy delta;
  flow scan (when did I last lose track of time? DFS-2-style reflection on 2
  activities); little-things practice (sensory detail of one mundane act);
  strengths inventory (what people ask me for; what comes effortlessly).
- **Outputs:** energy map; flow situations list; strengths list.
- **Action:** perform one activity this week in "little things" mode and note
  the difference.

### Stage 3 — Values & needs
- **Purpose:** the interior scaffolding; Kamiya's core contribution.
- **Teach:** ikigai-kan vs sources; the 7 needs + purpose (Kamiya flower);
  logotherapy in one card (meaning under all circumstances); Morita in one
  card (act before motivation).
- **Exercises:** **needs self-rating** (rate 7 needs 1–5, mark the 2 lowest);
  values clarification (pick from list + free text; rank top 5);
  "what do others expect of me?" separation exercise (write expectations of
  significant others, then set them beside own values);
  meaning audit (Frankl-style: what would make this week worth living even if
  nothing external changed?).
- **Outputs:** needs profile (persisted flower diagram data); top-5 values;
  expectation-vs-values contrast note.
- **Action:** one small act that serves a top value, done for its own sake.

### Stage 4 — Sources
- **Purpose:** name the user's actual ikigai (plural allowed, small allowed).
- **Teach:** sources can be people, hobbies, work, places, memories; they
  change; the feeling is the proof.
- **Exercises:** draft **sources list** (aim 5–12, seeded with candidates
  pulled forward from stages 1–3, editable); feelings vocabulary — for each
  source, pick/name the feelings it generates (ikigai-kan naming);
  two-word distillation (Tamashiro) as an *optional* compressive exercise,
  framed as a slogan, not a definition.
- **Outputs:** sources catalogue (source → feelings → needs served → stages
  that support it); optional two-word phrase.
- **Action:** schedule one source into the coming week that has been dormant.

### Stage 5 — Contribution & the world (with optional livelihood lens)
- **Purpose:** the relational/contribution dimension; and, for those who
  want it, connect ikigai to earning.
- **Teach:** resonance; Ikigai-9 subscale 3; the honest Venn history card
  (Zuzunaga → Winn) — then: "if you want to use this for work, here it is as
  a lens, not a definition."
- **Exercises:** world-needs reflection (what annoys me? what would I fix?
  who do I want to serve?); contribution inventory (where do I already give?);
  **optional livelihood lens**: apply love/good-at/world-needs/paid-for to
  *sources that want to grow*, producing career candidates and gaps; job
  crafting review (positivepsychology worksheet logic) of current role.
- **Outputs:** contribution list; (optional) livelihood map with gaps named;
  job-crafting changes list.
- **Action:** one contribution act (small, this week).

### Stage 6 — Enemies & experiments
- **Purpose:** clear resistance; convert insight into motion (Morita).
- **Teach:** Tamashiro's four enemies; fear-naming works; action precedes
  motivation.
- **Exercises:** enemies worksheet (failure / success / judgment / discomfort
  — write each, rate its grip, name one counter-move each);
  obstacle planning (if-then plans against top 2);
  **experiments plan**: 3 small experiments (one per chosen source/need),
  each with trigger, minimum version, and a check-in date.
- **Outputs:** enemies sheet; 3-experiment plan.
- **Action:** run experiment #1 (the plan *is* the action).

### Stage 7 — Synthesis & practice
- **Purpose:** converge everything into a durable artifact; close the loop;
  open the practice cycle.
- **Teach:** ikigai as ongoing practice; needs erode; sources rotate.
- **Exercises:** assemble **My Ikigai Map** (Mogi-style two-axis placement of
  sources; needs flower showing before/after; top values; named sources with
  feelings; optional two-word phrase; experiments); **Ikigai-9 retest** with
  delta vs baseline; revisit scheduler (pick a retest cadence).
- **Outputs:** the artifact (screen + print stylesheet + JSON/markdown
  export); retest delta card; practice plan.
- **Action:** export/print the artifact; put experiment check-ins somewhere
  real.

## 3. How depth is achieved (against the gap list)

- **G1 multi-session journey:** stage/step state machine, autosave, resume.
- **G2 depth + interactivity:** every exercise is one of the named
  methodologies (Kamiya needs, Mogi pillars, Mitsuhashi past-sifting,
  logotherapy audit, Morita journaling, Tamashiro enemies, job crafting),
  taught in 1–2 sourced cards before use.
- **G3 measurement:** Ikigai-9 baseline (stage 0) and retest (stage 7),
  subscale deltas.
- **G4 artifact:** the stage-7 map synthesises persisted outputs of every
  prior stage; printable; exportable.
- **G5 personalisation:** livelihood lens optional; needs-low areas get
  "strengthen" suggestions; stage 4 seeds from the user's own prior answers.
- **G6 practice loop:** revisit scheduler + retest delta; exercises reusable.
- **G7 privacy:** on-device storage; export/import; zero network calls after
  load.
- **G8 honesty:** inline citations; the Venn appears exactly once, labelled.

## 4. Open questions for the owner (also collected in docs/plan.md)

1. Single canonical journey vs alternate paths (career-forward users, retirees)?
2. Should the livelihood lens be shown by default or only on request?
3. English-only copy, or i18n-ready from the start?
4. Tone: calm/minimal vs warm/editorial (affects visual design direction)?
5. Is an anonymous local-only experience acceptable (no accounts, no stats)?
