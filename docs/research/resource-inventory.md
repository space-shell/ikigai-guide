# Phase 1 — Resource Inventory

Existing material on uncovering one's ikigai, surveyed 2026-11-17 via web search
and page reading. Format: title, creator, format, link, methodology/exercises,
strengths, weaknesses, depth rating (1 = surface/quiz, 5 = deepest available).

## 1. Books — original Japanese scholarship

### Ikigai ni Tsuite (生きがいについて, "On Ikigai") — Kamiya Mieko, 1966
- **Creator:** Kamiya Mieko (1914–1979), psychiatrist. Published by Misuzu Shobō;
  [archive.org record of the 2004 reprint](https://archive.org/details/ikigainitsuite0000kami).
- **Format:** Book (Japanese; no complete mainstream English translation).
- **Methodology/exercises:** Clinical research with leprosy patients at
  Nagashima Aiseien sanatorium, plus reviews of research on atomic-bomb
  survivors, terminal patients, death-row inmates and the bereaved. Produces
  the two-part definition that anchors everything below:
  - **ikigai** = the *sources* of meaning ("this child is my ikigai") — people,
    relationships, hobbies, dreams, memories.
  - **ikigai-kan** = the *feeling* that life is worth living produced by those
    sources — explicitly close to Viktor Frankl's "sense of meaning".
  - Seven **ikigai needs** whose satisfaction produces ikigai-kan: life
    satisfaction, change & growth, a bright future, resonance, freedom,
    self-actualization, meaning & value (see
    [Ikigai Tribe's breakdown](https://ikigaitribe.com/ikigai/ikigai-sources-ikigai-kan/)).
  - A separate **shimei-kan** (sense of mission/purpose), which Kamiya treated
    as above-and-beyond the needs; Kemp argues it functions as an eighth need.
- **Strengths:** The scholarly foundation every serious Japanese researcher
  still cites; gives a needs-based, feelings-first model that converts directly
  into exercises (rate your needs, name your sources, name your feelings).
- **Weaknesses:** Academic prose; no step-by-step self-guided program;
  inaccessible to English readers.
- **Depth: 5/5** — the deepest single source, but not itself a guided process.

### IKIGAI-KAN: Feel a Life Worth Living — Nicholas Kemp, 2022
- **Creator:** Nicholas Kemp, Japanologist, founder of
  [Ikigai Tribe](https://ikigaitribe.com/the-ikigai-kan-book/).
- **Format:** Book (~200pp) + extensive free companion content (podcast,
  worksheets, articles).
- **Methodology/exercises:** Synthesis of contemporary Japanese ikigai
  researchers (Kamiya lineage); presents Kamiya's 7 needs plus purpose as the
  ["8 ikigai needs"](https://x.com/NicholasKemp/status/1585040919619067904)
  ("Kamiya Flower" diagram); argues ikigai is plural, small-scale, everyday,
  and need not involve work or money; "chanto suru" (doing things properly)
  and action/mindfulness in daily life.
- **Strengths:** The most accessible *accurate* treatment in English; direct
  bridge from Kamiya to practical understanding; the Ikigai Tribe site hosts
  free [worksheets](https://ikigaitribe.com/ikigai/ikigai-worksheet/) and a
  [podcast](https://ikigaitribe.com/ikigai/podcast07/) with primary interviews
  (Zuzunaga, Mogi, Kamiya scholars).
- **Weaknesses:** Conceptual rather than programmatic — no staged journey or
  persistence of exercises; the structured worksheets are coach-facing.
- **Depth: 4/5.**

## 2. Books — popular treatments

### Ikigai: The Japanese Secret to a Long and Happy Life — García & Miralles, 2016
- **Format:** Book;
  [publisher page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/549469/ikigai-by-hector-garcia-and-francesc-miralles/).
- **Methodology/exercises:** Interviews with ~100 centenarians in Ogimi,
  Okinawa; Blue Zones framing; chapters on **logotherapy (Frankl)** and
  **Morita therapy** as practical routes to purpose
  ([ch. 3–4 summaries](https://www.supersummary.com/ikigai/chapters-3-4-summary/));
  flow (Csikszentmihalyi); anti-aging lifestyle; closing **10 rules of ikigai**
  (stay active, slow living, hara hachi bu, friends, movement, smile, nature,
  gratitude, present moment, follow your ikigai — see
  [this review](https://www.thisawfulawesomelife.com/home/2023/12/4/ikigai-the-japanese-secret-to-a-long-and-happy-life-by-francesc-miralles-and-hector-garcia)).
- **Strengths:** The best-selling entry point (millions of readers); the
  logotherapy/Morita chapter is a genuinely useful exercise source (values
  audit, action-before-motivation journaling); normalised "ikigai" as a word.
- **Weaknesses:** Conflates ikigai with Okinawan longevity folklore; lifestyle
  content (diet, movement) dominates the actual *discovery* process, which is
  thin; the four-circle diagram is associated with it in marketing despite
  barely appearing in the book; Kemp
  [documents the misframing](https://medium.com/ikigai-insights/ikigai-is-not-a-venn-diagram-cca7abba323)
  ("not from Okinawa, not a longevity secret").
- **Depth: 2.5/5** for discovery methodology; 4/5 for cultural reach.

### The Little Book of Ikigai (Awakening Your Ikigai) — Ken Mogi, 2017
- **Format:** Book; [Ikigai Tribe summary](https://ikigaitribe.com/books/the-little-book-of-ikigai/).
- **Methodology/exercises:** The **five pillars**:
  1. Starting small
  2. Releasing yourself (accept who you are)
  3. Harmony and sustainability
  4. The joy of little things
  5. Being in the here and now
  Illustrated through Japanese daily life (breakfast rituals, train conductors,
  ramen masters). Mogi also produced
  [a video](https://youtu.be/a_2RIydy_NQ) replacing the Venn diagram with a
  two-axis model (small↔big, private↔public) where **everything on the map is
  ikigai**, not just the centre ([context in Lee Byron's TIL](https://leebyron.com/til/ikigai/)).
- **Strengths:** Authored by a neuroscientist; the pillars convert cleanly into
  daily practice exercises; explicitly anti-"single grand purpose".
- **Weaknesses:** Meandering; anecdote-heavy; no structured program at all.
- **Depth: 3/5.**

### Ikigai: Giving Every Day Meaning and Joy — Yukari Mitsuhashi, 2018
- **Format:** Book (Kyle Books), referenced throughout
  [PositivePsychology.com's ikigai articles](https://positivepsychology.com/ikigai-worksheets-templates/).
- **Methodology/exercises:** Ikigai as everyday small joys, **not** a career
  question; sift your past for clues (school years onward); job crafting
  (log your day, reshape tasks); action over passivity; curiosity as fuel.
- **Strengths:** Closest mainstream book to the Japanese everyday conception;
  practical exercises (past-sifting, job crafting) that port directly to an
  interactive format.
- **Weaknesses:** Still prose-first; no measurement or progression.
- **Depth: 3/5.**

### How to Ikigai — Tim Tamashiro, 2019
- **Format:** Book + [TEDxYYC talk](https://www.ted.com/talks/tim_tamashiro_how_to_ikigai)
  (~970k views).
- **Methodology/exercises:** Simplified "treasure map": ikigai = **pleasure +
  mission**; the **two-word ikigai** exercise (distill to a verb+noun, e.g.
  "to delight"); **enemies of ikigai** — fear of failure, fear of success,
  fear of others' judgment, fear of discomfort.
- **Strengths:** The most *actionable* popular treatment; two-word distillation
  is a memorable synthesis exercise; enemies worksheet is a real insight.
- **Weaknesses:** Western-simplified; pleasure+mission is a personal branding
  of the concept, not the Japanese model.
- **Depth: 2/5.**

## 3. Academic research & validated measurement

### Ikigai-9 scale — Imai, Osada & Nishi 2012; English validation Fido, Kotera & Asano 2019
- **Format:** 9-item questionnaire; original in *Japanese Journal of Public
  Health* 59:433–440; validation: *Int. J. Mental Health and Addiction* 18,
  1352–1359, [doi:10.1007/s11469-019-00150-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11469-019-00150-w).
- **Methodology:** Three subscales ([coaching breakdown](https://ikigaitribe.com/ikigai/ikigai-worksheet/)):
  1. Emotional fulfillment & well-being (happy / mentally rich / room in mind)
  2. Growth & future orientation (develop myself / learn something new / interested in many things)
  3. Contribution & meaning (contributing / my existence is needed / have impact)
- **Strengths:** The only validated English ikigai measure; cheap to administer
  (9 items); ideal as baseline + retest to show progress.
- **Weaknesses:** Measures *present* ikigai-kan, not a discovery process.
- **Depth: 4/5** as an instrument.

### Health-outcome cohort studies (the evidence base)
- **[Sone et al. 2008, Ohsaki Study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18596247/)** —
  ~43,000 Japanese adults, 7-year follow-up: absence of ikigai associated with
  increased all-cause mortality (HR ≈ 1.5), especially cardiovascular.
- **[Tanno et al. 2009, Japan Collaborative Cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19539820/)** —
  ~73,000 adults: ikigai associated with decreased all-cause mortality risk.
- **[Mori et al. 2017, Tsurugaya Project](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpsychores.2017.02.013)** —
  ikigai associated with lower incident functional disability in elderly.
- **[Okuzono et al. 2022, Lancet Regional Health WP](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanwpc/article/PIIS2666-6065(22)00010-4/fulltext)** —
  ikigai associated with fewer depressive symptoms/hopelessness, higher
  happiness, life satisfaction and independence.
- **Strengths:** A genuinely evidence-backed concept — rare for self-help
  material. An interactive guide can cite these inline.
- **Weaknesses:** Observational cohorts; association ≠ causation; ikigai
  measured as single-item-ish sentiment, not as the Venn's constructs.
- **Depth: 5/5** as evidence; 0 as exercises.

### Adjacent validated instruments
- **Dispositional Flow Scale-2 (DFS-2)** — flow tendency, 9 dimensions
  ([overview](https://positivepsychology.com/ikigai-test-questionnaires/));
  useful as an exercise-level reflection ("when do I enter flow?").
- **Satisfaction With Life Scale (SWLS, Diener)** — 5-item life satisfaction;
  used in Japanese ikigai research (Kumano 2017).

## 4. Worksheets / printable PDFs

### PositivePsychology.com — "6 Worksheets & Templates to Find Your Ikigai"
- **Link:** <https://positivepsychology.com/ikigai-worksheets-templates/> (Sutton, 2021, updated 2026).
- **Methodology/exercises:** Job crafting day-log; strengthening ikigai in the
  workplace; identifying your ikigai (past-sifting questions); reflection on
  opposites (present/future, giving/receiving, growth/fixed); focus on the
  little things (sensory mindfulness); enemies of ikigai (Tamashiro's four
  fears); **20 questions** for the four circles (5 per circle); sentence-stem
  purpose completion ("When I was a child I loved…", "If I believed I could not
  fail…").
- **Strengths:** The densest free exercise library; each worksheet maps to a
  named methodology; clinically reviewed.
- **Weaknesses:** Static PDFs aimed at coaches; no progression, persistence,
  or personalisation; Venn-centric framing.
- **Depth: 3/5.**

### Ikigai Tribe — Ikigai-9 Coaching Pack & worksheets
- **Link:** <https://ikigaitribe.com/ikigai/ikigai-worksheet/>
- **Methodology/exercises:** Ikigai-9 scale worksheet + "Ikigai Map" +
  guided coaching script + walkthrough video; explicitly warns against the
  Western Venn chart; Kamiya Flower needs diagram.
- **Strengths:** Culturally accurate, evidence-based, free (email-gated).
- **Weaknesses:** Coach-facing; PDF-based; single measurement session.
- **Depth: 3/5.**

### Assorted printables
- [Etsy ikigai planners/journals marketplace](https://www.etsy.com/market/find_your_ikigai_pdf) —
  paid, unreviewed, Venn-templated.
- [Free printable Venn worksheet](https://islamimommy.com/2025/01/17/free-printable-ikigai-worksheet/)
  (islamimommy, 2025); [7-page coaching worksheet PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/625d6c9bde382d24d96870be/t/63d81500609abd0a39aa87c1/1675105539897/Ikigai+Worksheet.pdf)
  (Squarespace-hosted, coach-authored); [IKIGAI-Tool printable](https://www.scribd.com/document/674920787/IKIGAI-Tool) (Scribd).
- **Depth: 1–2/5** — form-filling with no methodological grounding.

## 5. Tests / questionnaires online

- **[ikigaitest.com](https://ikigaitest.com/)** — 10-minute online test oriented
  to career suggestions. Shallow scoring, one-shot, no persistence.
- **[People at Heart Coaching — Finding Your Ikigai](https://www.peopleatheartcoaching.com/finding-your-ikigai/)** —
  8 questions + downloadable purpose worksheet; career-oriented.
- **Ikigai-9 self-administration** (via the coaching pack or the published
  items) — validated but unformatted for lay users.
- **Depth: 1–2/5.**

## 6. Blogs / articles

- **[Marc Winn — "What is your Ikigai?"](https://theviewinside.me/what-is-your-ikigai/)**
  (14 May 2014) — the post that fused Zuzunaga's diagram with the word ikigai;
  now carries an honest [origin-story update](https://theviewinside.me/the-story-behind-the-ikigai-venn-diagram-a-personal-journey/)
  attributing the diagram. Depth 1/5; cultural impact enormous.
- **[Lee Byron — "til / ikigai"](https://leebyron.com/til/ikigai/)** (2022) —
  crisp critical genealogy of the meme + Mogi's replacement diagram. Depth 3/5.
- **[Nicholas Kemp — "Ikigai Is Not a Venn Diagram"](https://medium.com/ikigai-insights/ikigai-is-not-a-venn-diagram-cca7abba323)**
  (2020) + the [expanded PDF](https://spotlightonmentalhealth.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Ikigai-Misunderstood-and-the-Origin-of-the-Ikigai-Venn-Diagram.pdf)
  — etymology (iki = daily living; gai < kai, Heian-period shell value via the
  Kaiawase game), demolition of the "Okinawan secret" framing. Depth 4/5.
- **[Calm — "Ikigai: what it is and how to use it"](https://www.calm.com/blog/ikigai)**
  (2023) — 10 discovery tips (mindfulness, reflect on diagram, passions…).
  Depth 1.5/5, wellness-brand framing.
- **[everydayconcepts.io/ikigai](https://everydayconcepts.io/ikigai)** — concise
  concept explainer distinguishing the Venn from Kamiya. Depth 2/5.
- **[Ikigai Podcast](https://ikigaitribe.com/podcasts/)** (Kemp) — 90+
  episodes with Japanese researchers; e.g. ep. 7 (Zuzunaga), ep. 31 (Yoko
  Inoue on ikigai-kan), ep. 34 (Kamiya's life). Depth 4/5 reference material.

  saved locally") surfaced in search; **DNS no longer resolves as of
  2026-11-17** — evidence that hobby-grade attempts appear and die.
- **Depth: all 1–2/5.** None offers a multi-stage, saved-progress journey with
  methodological depth; the AI wrapper adds novelty, not method.
